All emoji designs are copyright OpenMoji contributors. They are used here,
unmodified, to illustrate picture-based quiz questions and lesson teaching
cards in the Manhaji app.

Library expanded 2026-07-03 to ~160 images (numbers, shapes, animals, fruits
and vegetables, food, family, school objects, body parts, clothes, weather
and nature, transport, home objects, and Islamic iconography) as part of the
clean-image system that replaced scanned textbook photos. Original 16 images
are 72x72; the expansion set is 618x618 for crisp rendering on teaching cards.
